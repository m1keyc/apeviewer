## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/m1keyc/apeviewer/releases/latest)**

- **APEViewerSetup.exe**: the installer (recommended).
- **APEViewer.exe**: the same app without installing.

Already installed? Click **Update** in the app; it updates itself from here.

## What it does

- See every screen of your other PC at once, each in its own window or full screen.
- Connect on your home network, over Tailscale, or from anywhere through APE Viewer's relays.
- Sound, a shared clipboard and controllers (with rumble).
- On the PC you share: see who's connected, switch each person's mouse, keyboard, controller, clipboard and sound live, kick people, or be asked before anyone connects.
- End-to-end encrypted. Your screens never pass through our servers in readable form.
